About ShallowSID

A C64 music player for the web

Music

All tunes come from the High Voltage SID Collection and its team, including the song lengths in Songlengths.md5.

Player engine

libsidplayfp and reSIDfp, built for WebAssembly by libsidplayfp-wasm. Licensed under the GNU General Public License, version 2 or later. Corresponding source of the build ShallowSID uses.

Libraries

  • Ionic for the interface.
  • MiniSearch for search.
  • DiceBear Critters (composer pages) and Patchwork (playlist covers), both CC0.
  • qrcode-generator and jsQR for sync key QR codes.
  • Press Start 2P font.
