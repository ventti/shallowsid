Privacy

ShallowSID is a free, non-commercial music player.

  • By default: your playlists, listening history and settings stay in your browser.
  • Sync is optional: it stores an encrypted copy online. Anyone with your sync key can read it.
  • Sharing is optional: anyone with a playlist link can read and copy that playlist.
  • Online requests: services that deliver the app and tunes receive technical data, including your IP address.

Data on your device

Your browser stores playlists, favorites, recent tunes and playlists, play counts, search history, sound presets and preferences. It also caches app files for offline use. Clear this site's data in your browser to remove these local copies. This does not delete online copies or data on other devices.

Sync

Sync is off by default. When enabled, it uploads your playlists, favorites, recent tunes and playlists, play counts, sound presets and sound settings. Search history stays on your device.

The encrypted copy also includes the devices using your sync key: random device IDs, public keys for secure key transfers, operating system, browser, installed-app status and last sync time. The place shown for a device comes from its time zone, not a location lookup. For curators, Sync also includes curator access details and private invite notes.

Your device encrypts this data before uploading it. ShallowSID's maintainer and storage provider cannot read its contents. Sync uses a random key. No account, name or email address is needed. Keep your sync key and its QR code private: anyone with the key can read, change or delete your synced data. If no device has the key anymore, we cannot recover it or the encrypted contents.

Sync links keep the key after #, which browsers do not send to the web server. The QR scanner processes camera images on your device and stops the camera when closed.

Deletion: Sync → Delete Synced Data deletes the current online copy when successful and turns Sync off on this device. Local data and shared playlists remain. Turning Sync off or clearing browser data alone does not delete the online copy. There is no scheduled removal of inactive copies.

Older records: removing a device changes the sync key. The old record keeps encrypted key-transfer data so remaining devices can reconnect; the app cannot delete that record. It no longer contains the old playlists or settings. Copies from before September 2026 also remain until the maintainer removes them manually, with no scheduled removal date. Delete Synced Data does not remove either kind of older record.

Sharing playlists

Live Link stores the playlist name and tune list online without end-to-end encryption and keeps them updated as you edit. It is not listed publicly, but anyone with the link can read it or pass it on. Avoid private information in playlist names.

The playlist stays online until you choose Stop Sharing. This removes its name and tunes, leaving a record that says it is no longer shared. Stop sharing before deleting local data if you want to remove the online playlist.

Snapshot Link puts a fixed copy of the playlist in the link itself, after #, without storing it in ShallowSID's database. You cannot revoke it. Neither sharing option can erase copies other people have saved.

Curators (invite only)

Ordinary listeners receive tune tags with the app files. Invited curators can edit tags and tempos. Online edit records contain the values, last-change time and a random curator ID. Changes can be linked to that ID, including through direct database access.

Curator records contain the ID, role, active status, access-verification data and source invite. Private invite notes stay in the admin's encrypted Sync data. Tags and tempos stay until changed; curator records, including deactivated ones, stay until the maintainer removes them.

Online services and security

GitHub Pages delivers the app, jsDelivr delivers libraries, and Google Firebase Hosting delivers tunes from the High Voltage SID Collection (HVSC). The official HVSC site is a fallback for tunes. Each service receives your IP address and request details, such as the file requested and browser information.

The maintainer can use Firebase request logs and usage totals to monitor traffic, downloads and service limits. Raw logs may contain visitor identifiers. The usage report keeps aggregate counts and tune paths, without IP addresses or other visitor identifiers. Downloads do not reliably show what you played, because your browser can reuse cached tunes.

Sync, live playlists and curator records use Google Cloud Firestore, with database storage in Finland (europe-north1). Their requests also use Firebase App Check with Google reCAPTCHA Enterprise to prevent automated abuse. This starts on the first database request and may keep refreshing while the app is open. Ordinary playback does not require these database features.

Google's bot protection processes device, browser and page-use data and uses a _grecaptcha security cookie. Google processes this data on our behalf under its data-processing terms and service terms.

Database storage in Finland does not mean all processing stays there. Providers may process technical data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Google's data-processing terms describe transfer safeguards, including European Commission standard contractual clauses where applicable, and how to obtain copies. For providers' processing and retention, see the GitHub privacy statement, jsDelivr privacy policy and Google Cloud privacy notice.

Why data is used

Data is used to provide the features you request, deliver the app and tunes, maintain tune information, monitor service use and prevent abuse. The GDPR legal basis for Sync and the curator role you accept is providing the service you request (Article 6(1)(b)). Delivering and protecting the service, monitoring usage and maintaining tune information rely on legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)).

Your rights and contact

Teppo Keitaanniemi runs ShallowSID and is its data controller. Contact shallowsid@extend.fi about privacy or your data.

Under the GDPR, you can request access, correction, deletion, restricted use or a portable copy of personal data, and object to use based on legitimate interests. These rights have legal conditions. We normally respond within one month; if more time is legally allowed, we explain why within that month.

Use Sync → Export User Data to download this device's current playlists, play history, sound settings, search history, Sync device list and any curator access details and invite notes. The file is unencrypted; keep it private. It does not include providers' request logs or older server records.

Use the app to view, change or delete encrypted Sync contents: we cannot read them for you. We may need information linking you to a record to handle other requests. If we cannot identify your data, GDPR Article 11 may limit what we can do.

You can complain to the Data Protection Ombudsman in Finland or the data protection authority where you live or work in the European Economic Area.
