Privacy

ShallowSID · last updated 2026-09-24

In short

ShallowSID works without accounts and without tracking. Unless you turn on Sync, nothing you do leaves your browser. If you turn it on, an encrypted copy of your playlists and settings is stored so your devices can share it. Nobody but your devices can read it.

Stored in your browser

  • Playlists, favorites, recently played tunes and playlists, play counts, sound presets and preferences, in this browser's local storage (keys starting with shallowsid.).
  • A copy of the app's own files and libraries, in the browser's cache, so it opens offline. Nothing about you is in it.
  • This is needed for features you use, stays on your device, and is never sent anywhere unless you turn on Sync. Clearing the site's data removes it.

Sync (optional, off by default)

  • What is stored: one encrypted record with your playlists, favorites, sound presets, two preferences (selected sound preset, pre-rendering) and the list of devices using the key: for each, its OS, browser, whether it runs as an installed app, when it last synced, and a place guessed from its time zone (such as “Helsinki”; no location services are used). It is encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM, using a key derived from your sync key. The record's ID is also derived from the sync key and reveals nothing about you. Recently played tunes and play counts are not synced.
  • What is not stored: no name, email, account or device identifier. The sync key itself never leaves your devices.
  • QR code: the sync key can be shown as a QR code (a link with the key after #, which browsers never send to a server). Scanning one in the app uses your camera only while the scanner is open; images are read on your device and never stored or sent.
  • Where: Google Cloud Firestore (Firebase), in the EU region europe-north1 (Finland), under Google's data processing terms. Like any web service, Google processes technical data such as IP addresses to deliver requests; ShallowSID does not collect or log them.
  • Why: to provide the sync you asked for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • How long: until you delete it with Sync → Delete Synced Data. It is not deleted automatically.
  • Your rights: Sync → Delete Synced Data removes the record immediately. Because the data is encrypted with a key only you hold, we cannot look it up, read it or recover it for you. Without the key it can't be linked to you.

Live links to playlists (optional)

  • What is stored: when you choose Share → Live Link, that playlist's name and the list of tunes are stored unencrypted, so that anyone with the link can open it. Nothing else is stored with it: no name, account or device identifier. A random proof value lets only your devices change it.
  • Who can see it: anyone who has the link. Links are not listed or searchable anywhere, but anyone you send one to can pass it on.
  • Where: Google Cloud Firestore, europe-north1 (Finland), as above.
  • How long: until you choose Stop Sharing. That removes the name and tunes at once; the link then only shows “no longer shared”. It is not deleted automatically.
  • Don't put personal information in the name of a playlist you share.

Other services

  • Tunes are downloaded from the High Voltage SID Collection (www.hvsc.c64.org) when you play them.
  • The app itself is served by GitHub Pages; libraries and fonts load from jsDelivr and Google Fonts. These services see your IP address when your browser fetches files from them.
